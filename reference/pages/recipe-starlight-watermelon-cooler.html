Starlight Watermelon Cooler

Ingredients

  • 0.75 oz Sweet African Watermelon syrup
  • 0.5 oz Persian Lime syrup
  • 10 oz soda water
  • 1 pinch silver or pink EDIBLE luster dust
  • Ice + mint
  • 1.5 pumps Sweet African Watermelon
  • 1 pump Persian Lime
  • 10 oz soda water
  • 1 pinch silver or pink EDIBLE luster dust
  • Ice + mint

Build

Shake syrups, soda and dust with ice to wake up the shimmer; pour over fresh ice with mint.

Swap itPersian Lime → Cherry Lime for a cherry-watermelon sparkle

Spike it1.5 oz vodka

Use food-grade luster dust labelled edible, with an ingredient list. Dust sold as "non-toxic" or for craft use is not for drinking. Stir or shake to suspend it — it settles — and keep the drink clear and iced; milk mutes it.
