Pumpkin Spice Martini

Syrups + Sauces + Toppings

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Vodka
  • 1/2 oz Stirling Great Pumpkin Spice Flavored Cane Sugar Syrup
  • 2 oz Chai Tea
  • Stirling Gourmet Caramel Sauce
  • Stirling Cinnamon Shakable Topping
  • 2 oz Vodka
  • 1/2 oz Stirling Great Pumpkin Spice Flavored Cane Sugar Syrup
  • 2 oz Chai Tea
  • Stirling Gourmet Caramel Sauce
  • Stirling Cinnamon Shakable Topping

Build

Rim Martini glass with Caramel Sauce Add Vodka, Syrup & Chai Tea in cocktail shaker shake over ice and strain into martini glass garnish with cinnamon shakable topping
