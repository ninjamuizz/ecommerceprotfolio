Toffee Macchiato

Syrups + Sauces

Ingredients

  • 1/2 oz Stirling English Toffee Flavored Cane Sugar Syrup
  • 2 oz espresso
  • 8 oz milk
  • 1 oz Stirling Gourmet Salted caramel sauce
  • 1/2 oz Stirling English Toffee Flavored Cane Sugar Syrup
  • 2 oz espresso
  • 8 oz milk
  • 1 oz Stirling Gourmet Salted caramel sauce

Build

Add Syrup in a 12 oz cup Stir and set aside. Steam milk in a pitcher, add hot milk & foam to cup Pour in espresso Add caramel sauce on top
