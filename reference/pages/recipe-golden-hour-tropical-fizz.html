Golden Hour Tropical Fizz

Syrups + luster dust2 products

SummerFruitTropicalShimmer

Ingredients

  • 0.75 oz Yellow Passionfruit syrup
  • 0.5 oz Polynesian Pineapple syrup
  • 10 oz soda water
  • 1 pinch gold EDIBLE luster dust
  • Ice
  • 1.5 pumps Yellow Passionfruit
  • 1 pump Polynesian Pineapple
  • 10 oz soda water
  • 1 pinch gold EDIBLE luster dust
  • Ice

Build

Shake the syrups and dust with ice to bloom the gold, then top with soda. Best in a tall clear glass in the light.

Swap itPolynesian Pineapple → Hawaiian Coconut for a golden pina-colada fizz

Spike it1.5 oz white rum

Use food-grade luster dust labelled edible, with an ingredient list. Dust sold as "non-toxic" or for craft use is not for drinking. Stir or shake to suspend it — it settles — and keep the drink clear and iced; milk mutes it.
