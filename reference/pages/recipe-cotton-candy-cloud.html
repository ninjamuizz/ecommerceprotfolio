Ingredients

  • 0.75 oz Bubble Gum syrup
  • 0.5 oz Cherry Blossom syrup
  • 10 oz soda water
  • 1 oz cream
  • Whipped cream
  • 1.5 pumps Bubble Gum
  • 1 pump Cherry Blossom
  • 10 oz soda water
  • 1 oz cream
  • Whipped cream

Build

Stir syrups into soda over ice, float the cream, pile on whip - it turns cotton-candy pink. A kids' / unicorn-menu drink.
