About

Made for the bar,
not the shelf.

Stirling has been family owned since 1993, and the formulation philosophy hasn't changed: pure cane sugar, real flavor concentration, no chemical finish. It's why most operators find they pour less Stirling per drink than what they were using before.

The lineup — cane sugar syrups, sugar-free syrups, gourmet sauces, frappe mixes, shakeable toppings, and tea concentrates — is built as one system, so flavors layer instead of fight. French vanilla into a cappuccino frappe. Peanut butter into a mocha. One bar, one supplier, no dead ends.

If a case doesn't meet spec, we replace it or credit it. No forms, no argument.

Quality Guaranteed

Thirty-three years in, we've learned that operators don't switch suppliers over a catalog — they switch over who picks up the phone. Reorder, spec sheet, or problem: you get a person, same day.

Six lines that work together.

Built for coffee houses: coffee goes in first, then the flavor. The same lines work in a dirty soda, a tea or a frappe.
